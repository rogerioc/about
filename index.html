About

Engineering judgment, applied to AI

I'm a senior software engineer with 20+ years building and shipping production systems, now making a deliberate move into Applied AI. Over the past year I've gone deep — completing a postgraduate program in AI Engineering (2025) plus hands-on projects in LLM applications, retrieval-augmented search, and on-device model inference. I work across the full stack of an AI feature: prompt and context engineering, structured model outputs, the backend services that run them, and the mobile clients that deliver them.

For over a decade I architected high-traffic fintech and marketplace mobile platforms, leading teams and setting technical direction. That production background is exactly what lets me build AI that holds up in real systems, not only in notebooks.

I hold an MSc in Software Engineering and a MEC-recognized postgraduate specialization in AI Engineering, have taught at both undergraduate and postgraduate level, and have five peer-reviewed publications in software engineering.
